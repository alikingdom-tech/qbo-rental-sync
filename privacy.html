This Privacy Policy explains what information APP_NAME (the "App") accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected. The App is a private tool operated by Yosemite Partners LLC ("we", "us") to automate bookkeeping for our own rental properties.

1. Information the App accesses

  • QuickBooks Online data: through Intuit's API, with your authorization, the App reads and creates accounting records in your QuickBooks Online company. This includes the chart of accounts, classes and locations, vendors, customers (tenants), products and services, and transactions such as deposits, bills, expenses, and journal entries.
  • Property management reports: PDF statements you provide (for example, monthly owner statements). The App reads them so it can create the matching entries.
  • Authorization tokens: the OAuth 2.0 tokens Intuit issues so the App can connect to your company. The App never sees or stores your QuickBooks password.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to create and check accounting entries in your QuickBooks Online company. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. The only exceptions are when required by law, or when it is processed by the infrastructure providers that run the App (such as cloud hosting) under their own security and confidentiality terms.

4. Storage and security

  • All communication with QuickBooks Online uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • OAuth tokens and credentials are stored in a protected location that only the App owner can access, and they are never written to logs or public code repositories.
  • The App keeps only what it needs to run, such as a mapping of record IDs and processing logs. It keeps no separate copy of your accounting data beyond that.
  • The App requests only the QuickBooks accounting permission it needs (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting).

5. Retention and deletion

Uploaded reports and processing logs are kept only as long as needed to complete and check the entries. When you disconnect the App from QuickBooks Online, its access is revoked, and the stored tokens and related working data are deleted. You can also ask for deletion at any time using the contact below.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time from QuickBooks Online (Apps → My Apps) or by contacting us. Records the App already created in QuickBooks stay in your QuickBooks company, under your control.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for and does not collect information from children.
