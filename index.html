Private bookkeeping automation for rental properties

Accounting Automation is a private tool that reads property management reports (such as monthly owner statements in PDF form) and records the matching transactions in QuickBooks Online. It saves the time spent on manual data entry and keeps the books for each rental property consistent.

How it works

  1. You connect your QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's secure sign-in (OAuth 2.0).
  2. The App reads the property management report: rent collected, management fees, repairs, owner draws, and other items.
  3. It creates the matching entries in QuickBooks, using your existing accounts, properties, and vendors.
  4. You review the entries in QuickBooks as usual.
Private app. APP_NAME is not available to the public or listed on the Intuit App Store. Only QuickBooks companies authorized by its owner can use it.

Connecting and disconnecting

The App is connected to QuickBooks by its owner. To disconnect it, go to QuickBooks Online → Apps → My Apps, find APP_NAME, and choose Disconnect.
